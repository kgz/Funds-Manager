# Liabilities

Loans and other debts you track in Funds Manager - home loans, car loans, credit cards, and other facilities.

Open it from the sidebar under **Net worth**: **Liabilities**.

![Liabilities page with total owed in the header and a table of home and car loans with balances, rates, and repayments](/screenshots/liabilities/list.png)

## Add a liability

1. Click **Add liability**.
2. Enter a **Name**, **Type**, and **Lender**.
3. Enter the **Current balance ($)** and optional loan terms (rate, repayment, term, linked account).
4. Click **Add liability**.

Credit limit fields appear for credit cards and buy-now-pay-later types.

![Add liability dialog with name, type, lender, current balance, and optional rate and repayment fields](/screenshots/liabilities/add.png)

## Edit and balance history

Open **Edit** on a row to update metadata (rate, repayment, linked account, notes). To change the balance shown in the table, add a snapshot under **Balance history** - the newest date wins.

![Edit liability dialog showing balance history with current and past snapshots plus fields to add a new balance](/screenshots/liabilities/edit.png)

## Linked account

**Linked account** is the offset or repayment account, when you have one on [Accounts](/user-guide/accounts). It is for your records - it does not sync balances automatically.

## Effects

- **Effect:** liability balances roll into **debts** on the Dashboard net worth chart.
- **Effect:** loans listed here appear when you add or edit property on [Assets](/user-guide/assets) or when you create loan-related plans in [Planning](/user-guide/planning).
- **Effect:** repayments on included loans feed [Serviceability](/user-guide/serviceability) commitments and are frozen into [Report snapshots](/user-guide/report-snapshots).
