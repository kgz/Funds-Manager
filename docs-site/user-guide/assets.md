# Assets

What you own outside everyday bank balances - property, super, vehicles, and other valuations that feed net worth.

Open it from the sidebar under **Net worth**: **Assets**.

![Assets page with total value in the header and a table of property and super entries with type badges and valuation dates](/screenshots/assets/list.png)

## Add an asset

1. Click **Add asset**.
2. Enter a **Name** and **Type** (property, super, vehicle, and so on).
3. Enter **Current value ($)**, **Valued as at**, and optionally a **Valuation source**.
4. For property, optionally pick a **Linked loan** from [Liabilities](/user-guide/liabilities).
5. Click **Add asset**.

Purchase price and date are optional - they are for your own cost-base notes.

![Add asset dialog with name, type, current value, valued-as-at date, and optional linked loan for property](/screenshots/assets/add.png)

## Edit and valuation history

Open **Edit** on a row to change the name, type, linked loan, or notes. To change the dollar value, add a row under **Valuation history** - the newest date becomes the table value.

A **Stale** badge appears when the valuation is missing or older than twelve months.

![Edit asset dialog showing valuation history with current and past entries plus fields to add a new valuation](/screenshots/assets/edit.png)

## Effects

- **Effect:** asset values roll into **total assets** on the Dashboard net worth chart.
- **Effect:** linking property to a loan ties the register to [Liabilities](/user-guide/liabilities) for your own reference (it does not recalculate the loan balance).

Everyday cash in imported bank accounts stays on [Accounts](/user-guide/accounts) - use **Savings (external)** here only when you want that balance in net worth without importing statements for it.
